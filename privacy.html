Privacy Policy

TX Charge Codes · Effective October 7, 2026

TX Charge Codes ("the app") is a reference tool for looking up Texas offense and charge codes. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Short version

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few things only on your own device so it works the way you left it: your favorite charges, your display setting (such as dark mode), and the most recently downloaded copy of the charge code list. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Update check

The app can check for updated charge code data. When you use "Check for updates," the app downloads a public data file (codes.json) hosted on GitHub Pages. No personal information is sent by the app with this request. As with any website request, the hosting service (GitHub) may receive standard technical information such as your IP address; the app does not receive or use it. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Spanish phrase audio

The Spanish phrase feature uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device to read phrases aloud. The app does not record audio and does not use your microphone.

Permissions

The app only needs internet access, used for the update check described above. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Not legal advice, not a government app

This app is an independent reference tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency. Charge code information may be incomplete or out of date. Always verify against the current Texas statutes and your agency's guidance before relying on it.

Children

The app is intended for adults, such as law enforcement and criminal justice professionals, and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
